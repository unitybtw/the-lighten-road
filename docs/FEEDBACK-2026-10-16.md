# Ember and lantern prototype feedback — 2026-10-16

Furkan's playtest feedback calls for scarce pickups, gradual Dim/Flare transitions, a quieter HUD suited to a decaying ruin, and a two-use ember that communicates its lifetime through the stone itself.

## Prototype changes

- The archive test chamber now has three pickups at x=9.5, x=26.7, and x=46.5, matching the proposed 2–3 pickups per stage. This is a test layout, not final level balance.
- Flare still costs one charge and lasts six seconds. The default 0.65-second fade in and fade out are included in those six seconds. Light radius, intensity, sparks, and ember wear follow the same gameplay timeline.
- The first use fades a full ember from 100% toward 50%; the second use darkens it from 50% toward 0% while fractures appear. Only the second completed use triggers the short break feedback and removes the exhausted icon.
- Manual early release finishes with a gradual fade and still spends the reserved charge. Another scout cannot start during that release. Death/restart clears the burn presentation without pretending an ember broke.
- The HUD removes the numeric countdown, luminous timer line, charge pips, large charge counter, key box, and empty sockets. Muted stone/ochre colors and rough asymmetric placeholder stones replace the brighter geometric presentation. The burning ember is the progress indicator; motion reduction preserves this information while suppressing decorative movement.
- `LanternHUD` exposes full, half, and fracture sprite slots plus a break audio clip slot. These are ready for Maksim's final artwork and final sound. Current icons and the synthesized crack are explicitly prototype placeholders. Concept sheets remain unchanged and are not presented as finished game sprites.
- The background remains prototype blockout scenery. Final background art direction is still for the artists and designer to establish.

## Integration contract

The shared `LanternFuel` API is unchanged. A scout reserves its one charge at activation, preserving immediate inventory availability for machinery code. The HUD continues to display that reserved stone while it burns. `HasActiveScout` includes the dimming tail, `FlareBlend` describes current illumination, and `ScoutProgress` describes remaining use. `IsFlared` describes the requested mode; it is false during a manual dimming tail. `EmberBroken` is a completion event, not an activation event. `ResetToDim()` resets presentation for retry/respawn.

Changes are limited to the lantern/HUD and related Siraç-owned test/prototype systems. Kemal's sandbox and mechanism files are untouched.

## Verification

Regression checks cover gradual radius transitions, both ember uses, the last available charge, early release, reset without false break feedback, and pickup collection during an active burn. The map checks still exercise the complete route, checkpoint recovery, and restart with all three pickups restored. All **59 Play Mode tests passed locally in Unity 6000.3.2f1 through Unity MCP** (0 failed, 0 skipped) on 2026-10-16; hosted Unity testing still depends on CI license activation.
